# superknight mesh: source and licence

`src/modes/superknight-mesh.js` (4,708 vertices, 9,574 triangles) is generated, not downloaded.

1. Concept image: `scripts/superknight-pipeline/k34-concept.jpg`, a kneeling knight in a
   three-quarter front view (hooded helm, sword planted, arrow in his back, cloak), generated by
   seb with OpenAI gpt-image (image-gen skill), 2026-11-16. Output owned by the requester under
   OpenAI's terms.
2. Image to 3D: Tencent Hunyuan3D-2 (`tencent/Hunyuan3D-2`, shape `hunyuan3d-dit-v2-0` plus the
   paint/texture model), run locally on an RTX 4080 (`shape.py`, `tex.py`). No paid API.
   Licence: Tencent Hunyuan 3D 2.0 Community License Agreement. Outputs may be used freely; the
   licence does not cover the EU, the UK or South Korea, and products with more than 1M MAU need
   a separate licence from Tencent. https://github.com/Tencent/Hunyuan3D-2/blob/main/LICENSE
3. Bake (`bake.py`): 1.2M-face raw mesh -> 60k textured -> decimated to 9,574 triangles with
   fast-simplification, vertex colours sampled from the texture, each vertex tagged with a
   material (steel, mail, cloak, leather, linen, blade) and a group (legs, body, head, sword,
   cloak), quantised to u16 positions. `scripts/bake-superknight-mesh.mjs` wraps the binary
   into the JS module.
